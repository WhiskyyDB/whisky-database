The whisky auction price index

Across the 29 distilleries with an unbroken auction record since 2006, the mean winning bid rose from £53 in 2006 to a peak of £263 in 2022, a 5.0x increase. It has since eased 22.4% to £204 in 2024 (Jan to Oct), still 3.86x its 2006 level. The single strongest month was 2022-05 at £370.

Which distilleries command the highest prices

Yamazaki tops the 2024 index at £678 per lot, ahead of Glenfarclas (£404) and Bowmore (£389). Over the last ten years Woodford Reserve gained the most, +253.8% from £37 to £130, while Oban moved -7.0%.

How strong the bottlings are

Where the source publishes a real ABV, the median bottling is 50.0% and 54.9% are bottled at 50% or above, cask-strength territory. The strongest on record is 70.0%. By type, Single Malt Scotch averages the highest at 51.5%.

94.3% of producers with a known founding year were founded in 2010 or later, 656 companies in under two decades, against 5 founded before 1900.

Method, reuse and citation

  • Source. The full WhiskyDB snapshot of 2026-10-02, built entirely from open sources: the US TTB COLA label registry, UK Companies House, the EU eAmbrosia GI register, Open Food Facts, Wikipedia and WhiskyHunter's open auction statistics. Every row carries a provenance ledger entry; see Sources & licenses.
  • Coverage is uneven by design. Open registries do not all publish every attribute. Every figure above names its denominator, so a percentage is always a share of the records that state that attribute, never of the whole catalogue. Blank fields are never guessed, and the documented 40% ABV fallback is excluded from every ABV figure.
  • Producers, not appellations. The dataset also lists the 273 protected spirit appellations of the EU GI register. An appellation is a protected place of origin, not a company, so every producer figure on this page leaves them out; data.json reports them apart as totals.gi_appellations.
  • The auction index is a market index. Each value is a distillery-level mean winning bid for one month, never the price of a specific bottle. Yearly figures average the distilleries equally so a distillery with many linked bottlings does not dominate.
  • Refresh. The catalogue is refreshed monthly; this page and its charts are regenerated after each refresh, so figures move. Cite the snapshot date.
  • Reuse. The figures and charts on this page are published under CC BY 4.0: use them in articles, slides and posts with a link to https://whiskydb.dataengineered.io/stats/. The machine-readable version is data.json. Figures derived from WhiskyHunter, Companies House and Wikipedia retain those sources' attribution terms. The underlying row-level dataset is a separate commercial product.
