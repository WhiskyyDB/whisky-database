# Changelog

All notable changes to the WhiskyDB dataset snapshots.

> Counts are stated as **minimums** (e.g. `1,290+`). The dataset is refreshed
> monthly (automated label-registry ingestion), so the live figures only grow —
> the numbers below stay accurate between snapshots.

- **"At least" for the stated-ABV share; "producers & brands"** (2026-10-04): README and `DATA_DICTIONARY.md` said 22% of spirits carry an "explicitly sourced" ABV. That figure is the share whose ABV differs from 40.0, and a 40.0 can be stated by the source too, which the 2026.10 edition cannot tell apart from the documented default, so the share is a lower bound: both now say "at least 22%" (README table "≥22%"). The ABV section of `/stats/` described its 709 bottlings as those whose ABV is "explicitly published by the source"; it now says they are the bottlings whose ABV differs from the 40% default, and that a stated 40% is left out with the default. The producer rows are now called **producers & brands** (homepage hero, title descriptions, structured data, README headline, badge and table, `llms.txt`, `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`, `/stats/`): the US label registry, Open Food Facts and the Wikipedia brands list give brand names, not producers. The count (2,720) is unchanged. `scripts/generate_stats.py` reads `spirits.abv_source` when the database has it (from the 2026.11 edition, which records where each ABV comes from): its ABV figures then count the ABVs stated by the source, a stated 40% included, and `stats/data.json` says which rule applied (`abv.rule`). `scripts/check_claims.py` follows the new sentences, and it now fails while a sentence that is true only up to the 2026.10 edition is still on the site for a newer one: "N of the M spirits have an ABV other than 40.0" (once `abv.rule` counts the stated ABVs) and the 750/700 mL volume-default disclosure (once `stats/data.json` `totals.volume_stated`, new, is below the spirit count), so the next re-sync rewords them. es/de/fr/pt-br copies translated. No figure changes.
